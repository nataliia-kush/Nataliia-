Meet the artist

Nataliia Kushnir

Fashion sees
the details.
Illustration keeps them.

With ten years of experience as an illustrator, I’ve worked with clients around the world on digital illustration projects and children’s books. My background as a model and choreographer has shaped the way I see fashion: through silhouette, movement and presence. Today, fashion illustration brings those worlds together. I create full-figure illustrations that celebrate the individuality and detail of each look.
