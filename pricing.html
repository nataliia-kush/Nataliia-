Live fashion illustrations are drawn for your guests during the event. Here is what the booking includes, how timing works and what to expect when you enquire.

Live event illustration

Pricing

Minimum booking · first 3 hours

R6,000

Each additional hour

R2,000

A R2,000 deposit secures your date. It is credited toward the booking total if the event goes ahead; if the booking is cancelled, the deposit is non-refundable.

What to expect

Thoughtful details,
from first line to handover.

Illustrations

Full-colour, full-figure fashion illustrations on standard A5 paper. I can draw approximately 6–8 individual guest illustrations per hour.

Before the event

We confirm the date, venue and event details in advance. I send you a booking agreement to review and sign. Your date is confirmed once I receive the signed agreement and R2,000 deposit. I arrive 20–30 minutes before the booked illustration time to set up.

Packaging

Each finished illustration is placed in a protective plastic sleeve before it is handed to the guest, helping protect it from spills and wear.

Travel

Travel for events more than 50 km from Hout Bay is quoted separately. Any travel cost is confirmed with you before the booking is finalised.

The couple’s keepsake

A more detailed full-figure illustration of the bride and groom is created in my studio after the wedding. It is included in the event service and delivered to you after the celebration.

Commissions

Fashion portraits and wedding stationery illustrations are created to order. Pricing depends on the artwork, intended use and any print requirements.
